With You

With You is a calm, supportive space for moments of overwhelm, task paralysis, and getting started. Designed for ADHD brains — diagnosed or self-identified — and anyone who’s ever struggled at the starting line.

What it does Design principles

Built by

This project is intentionally calm: fewer features, clearer choices, and support that feels human.

Want to help test? • Get in touch

What it does

Capture without pressure

Get things out of your head quickly, then decide what matters now — without turning your brain into an inbox you can’t keep up with.

Choose one next step

Reduce the moment of “where do I even start?” into a single, doable action. The goal is a first step, not a perfect plan.

When it helps

  • Overwhelm and task paralysis
  • Starting friction
  • Too many open loops
  • Gentle structure for focus
  • “I just need someone with me” moments

Design principles

Calm over noise

We keep the interface quiet so you can hear yourself think.

Support, not judgment

No streaks, shame, or “you failed” messaging.

Start small

A first step is success. Momentum comes later.
