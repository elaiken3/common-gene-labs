Beliefs

Starting is often the hardest part

We design for the moment before momentum—when your brain is loud and the first step feels impossible.

Difficulty isn’t failure

We treat friction as information, not a moral verdict. Systems should adapt to people—not the other way around.

Calm beats optimization

We avoid pressure-based design. The goal is steadiness, clarity, and emotional safety.

What we make

Small, thoughtful tools that reduce friction without adding pressure. We build in public with care, curiosity, and respect for how people actually work.

Design principles

  • Default to less
  • Explain why things exist
  • Design for hard days
  • Support variability, not ideals
  • Be a companion, not a coach

What you won’t see

  • Hustle language
  • Shame loops
  • “Fix yourself” framing
  • Dark patterns or pressure metrics
  • Noise for the sake of engagement
