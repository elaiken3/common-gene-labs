# Common Gene Labs — Values

Common Gene Labs builds human-centered tools for starting, focus, and emotional friction.

We design for real humans, especially on hard days.

## What we believe
- Starting is often the hardest part.
- Difficulty isn’t failure. It’s information.
- Calm beats optimization.
- Systems should adapt to people, not the other way around.

## How we build
- Default to less.
- Explain why things exist.
- Design for variability, not ideals.
- Avoid pressure-based design.
- Build with people, not at them.

## What we will not do
- No shame loops.
- No hustle language.
- No dark patterns.
- No manipulative engagement mechanics.
- No “fix yourself” framing.

## Product bar
A feature ships only if it:
- reduces friction,
- increases clarity,
- or increases emotional safety,
without adding pressure.

## Voice & tone
We speak:
- calmly,
- precisely,
- without judgment,
- without excessive cheer.

We do not speak like:
- a coach,
- a productivity guru,
- or a compliance system.

## Privacy & respect
We minimize data collection.
If we track something, we should be able to say why in one sentence.
We do not treat users as metrics.
