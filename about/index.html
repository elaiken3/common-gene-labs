About

Common Gene Labs

Common Gene Labs is an independent lab exploring gentler systems for being human — especially for people who struggle with starting, focus, and overwhelm.

We build calm, supportive tools that reduce friction without adding pressure. The goal isn't optimization. It's steadiness, clarity, and emotional safety.

"Common Gene" is a reminder that difficulty is not rare or exceptional — it's shared. Our work starts from that truth.

Eugene (Gene) Lawrence Aiken III

I'm a Senior Data Engineer and the founder of Common Gene Labs. I build tools that feel like support — not a system you have to perform for.

I created With You for moments when starting feels hard, shaped by lived experience with ADHD, overwhelm, and the quiet difficulty of taking a first step.

I built Tend: Recovery Care at the beginning of 2026 after simultaneously supporting three family members through major surgeries. The tool I needed didn't exist, so I made it.

Economics, Columbia University. Brother of Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity, Inc. Passionate about coding and technology education for Black youth and children of color.
